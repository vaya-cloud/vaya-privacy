2. Data processing in the app

VAYA does not require an account. Entries, exercise history and settings are stored locally on the device. VAYA operates no server for this data and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking services. Uninstalling the app deletes this local app data.

3. Apple Health

Only if you explicitly enable the feature, VAYA can save the duration and time of completed exercises as mindful minutes in Apple Health. VAYA does not read health data. Health data is not used for advertising or profiling and is not transferred to VAYA’s provider. You can manage or delete permissions and saved entries in Apple Health at any time.

4. Reminders

Optional exercise reminders are scheduled on your device and delivered by the operating system. VAYA does not use a push server for them. You can change notification permission in system settings.

5. On-device features

If VAYA uses Apple Intelligence features, processing takes place through Apple’s system frameworks on supported devices. VAYA does not transmit the content used by these features to its provider’s own server.

If you email us, we process your email address and the information you provide to respond. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns contractual or pre-contractual matters, and otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is responding to your message. We delete the data when it is no longer required for this purpose and no statutory retention obligation applies.
